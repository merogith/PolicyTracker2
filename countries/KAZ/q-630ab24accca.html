Action claims are statements tagged as commitments, planned actions, underway or delivered. These are documented statements, not independent verification of delivery.

Future risks are projected to rise, particularly for floods and heatwaves. Under an intermediate emissions scenario, SSP2-4.5 ¹, high-hazard flood risks may triple and long-duration heatwaves could increase six-fold by 2100, with even higher risks under high-emission pathways. Based on historic loss patterns and climate projections, cumulative damages for sudden onset events between 2026-2035 are estimated at USD 1.5–5 billion”
