Kazakhstan describes climate-policy governance and mainstreaming through inter-ministerial coordination. NDC 3.0 describes a whole-of-government approach translating national strategies into sector targets and investment priorities, with stakeholder engagement; a national working group was established as the main decision-making and coordination platform. The Ministry of Ecology and Natural Resources has coordinated climate policy with sectoral ministries since 2019.
