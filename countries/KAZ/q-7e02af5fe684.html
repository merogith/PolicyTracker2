About these scores

Claims receive 0–4 points for their reported stage. Each distinct claim counts once. Scoring methodology

Each topic averages its action claims: Commitment 25, Planned 50, Underway 75, Delivered 100. Topic methodology

Action claims are statements tagged as commitments, planned actions, underway or delivered. These are documented statements, not independent verification of delivery.

Counts use individual source findings. Exact restatements with the same figures and statement type count once across documents. Different wording or progression states remain separate. A claim counts once nationally and may appear in several topics or domains. Do not add those rows together.

  • National MRV System Uses Inventory and Remote SensingThe national MRV system relies on the greenhouse-gas inventory, with remote sensing and GIS supporting data; quality standards and validation protocols are to be developed to improve reporting consistency and comparability.Context0 points1 document1 quote1 supporting finding

    The national MRV system is based on the National Greenhouse Gas Inventory maintained by the Ministry of Ecology and Natural Resources. Remote sensing and GIS support reliable inventory data; quality standards and validation protocols are to be developed to improve consistency, reliability and comparability and support integrated climate reporting.

    • KAZ-NDC1 · p. 64Text matched
      “The core of the national MRV system is the National Greenhouse Gas Inventory, maintained by the Ministry of Ecology and Natural Resources. Remote sensing (RS) and geographic information system (GIS) data play a key role within the NIR system. The use of RS and GIS as tools for NIR ensures the generation of reliable and objective data. Their comprehensive integration into monitoring and reporting processes enables the generation of long-term and comparable environmental data, and their consistent application creates the basis for transparent and verifiable national reporting, particularly for the National Greenhouse Gas Inventory. In this regard, unified sets of quality standards (quality assurance/quality control) and data validation protocols will be developed to ensure the consistency, reliability, and comparability of information used in national monitoring, planning, and reporting systems.”
