About these scores

Claims receive 0–4 points for their reported stage. Each distinct claim counts once. Scoring methodology

Each topic averages its action claims: Commitment 25, Planned 50, Underway 75, Delivered 100. Topic methodology

Action claims are statements tagged as commitments, planned actions, underway or delivered. These are documented statements, not independent verification of delivery.

Counts use individual source findings. Exact restatements with the same figures and statement type count once across documents. Different wording or progression states remain separate. A claim counts once nationally and may appear in several topics or domains. Do not add those rows together.

  • Industrial modernization faces investment constraintsThe NDC identifies limited capacity to mobilize capital and invest in low-carbon technologies and innovation as a barrier to faster modernization and emissions reduction.Concern0 points1 document2 quotes1 supporting finding

    The document says that investment in fixed capital is insufficient to support expanded production and that, given high marginal costs and planned economic growth, the country’s ability to mobilize capital and provide additional investment in low-carbon technologies and innovation is limited. It also says Belarus needs more active development and deployment of low-carbon technologies and innovation, especially in sectors where emissions remain high, and needs technical support and investment to implement reduction measures and pursue more ambitious goals. This is presented as a constraint and support need, not as secured investment or a specific funded industrial programme.
