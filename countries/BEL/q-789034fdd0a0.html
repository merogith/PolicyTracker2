About these scores

Claims receive 0–4 points for their reported stage. Each distinct claim counts once. Scoring methodology

Each topic averages its action claims: Commitment 25, Planned 50, Underway 75, Delivered 100. Topic methodology

Action claims are statements tagged as commitments, planned actions, underway or delivered. These are documented statements, not independent verification of delivery.

Counts use individual source findings. Exact restatements with the same figures and statement type count once across documents. Different wording or progression states remain separate. A claim counts once nationally and may appear in several topics or domains. Do not add those rows together.

  • Emissions projections use inventories and reportsEmissions projections draw on inventory reports, biennial reporting and national communications; ongoing inventory improvements may change time-series indicators, including the 1990 base year.Context0 points1 document3 quotes1 supporting finding

    The NDC identifies the National Inventory Report for 1990–2018, the Fourth Biennial Report (2019) and national communications as basic data sources for greenhouse-gas emissions projections. It says the greenhouse-gas inventory process is being continuously improved and that this may change time-series indicators, including the 1990 base year. The projections were calculated following the 2006 IPCC Guidelines for National Greenhouse Gas Inventories and parameters used to prepare inventories.
