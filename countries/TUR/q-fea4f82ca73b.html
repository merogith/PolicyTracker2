About these scores

Claims receive 0–4 points for their reported stage. Each distinct claim counts once. Scoring methodology

Each topic averages its action claims: Commitment 25, Planned 50, Underway 75, Delivered 100. Topic methodology

Action claims are statements tagged as commitments, planned actions, underway or delivered. These are documented statements, not independent verification of delivery.

Counts use individual source findings. Exact restatements with the same figures and statement type count once across documents. Different wording or progression states remain separate. A claim counts once nationally and may appear in several topics or domains. Do not add those rows together.
