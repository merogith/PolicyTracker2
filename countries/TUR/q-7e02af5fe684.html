About these scores

Claims receive 0–4 points for their reported stage. Each distinct claim counts once. Scoring methodology

Each topic averages its action claims: Commitment 25, Planned 50, Underway 75, Delivered 100. Topic methodology

Action claims are statements tagged as commitments, planned actions, underway or delivered. These are documented statements, not independent verification of delivery.

Counts use individual source findings. Exact restatements with the same figures and statement type count once across documents. Different wording or progression states remain separate. A claim counts once nationally and may appear in several topics or domains. Do not add those rows together.

  • Türkiye Sets 2035 Emissions Reduction Against BAUUsing 2018 as its reference year, Türkiye’s NDC aims to cut emissions by 466 Mt CO₂ equivalent against BAU, limiting 2035 emissions to 643 Mt; further methodological details are due in its next BTR.Commitment+1 point1 document2 quotes1 supporting finding

    Türkiye identifies 2018 as its reference year and uses it as the baseline for future emissions pathways and mitigation efforts. It developed 2035 business-as-usual and mitigation scenarios using 2018 inventory data and the CLEWs modelling framework. The NDC target is to reduce emissions by 466 Mt CO₂ equivalent against the BAU scenario, limiting emissions to 643 Mt CO₂ equivalent in 2035. Further methodological details and sector breakdowns are to be provided in the next Biennial Transparency Report.
