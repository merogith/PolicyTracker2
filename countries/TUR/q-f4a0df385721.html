Türkiye has established climate-planning and governance arrangements: mitigation and adaptation plans serve as NDC implementation instruments and are to be revised as necessary; the Directorate of Climate Change coordinates climate policy and NDC preparation, with the Coordination Board providing an inter-ministerial and multi-stakeholder platform. The Climate Portal tracks progress against indicators, and provinces are required to prepare local climate action plans, supported by defined procedures and provincial coordination boards.
