# Current scoring method

**The authoritative scoring proposal is [UI/scoring revision 4.0](../ui-scoring/METHOD.md).** Its [editable settings](../ui-scoring/scoring.json), [reference calculator](../ui-scoring/scoring.mjs) and [worked examples](../ui-scoring/examples.json) are maintained together.

The dashboard shows unique claim counts, eligible response scores, topic averages, domain averages and a country average over the fixed topic profile. Four score colors and comparable-change arrows explain these same values. Context is unscored; reported implementation, contradictions and source availability remain separate facts.

The earlier coverage-only headline and topic-band method are historical. They remain in the preserved v3 archive and legacy reference tests, not as a competing current recommendation. The [updated plan](DECISION.md) describes integration without a scoring LLM or a human review queue.
