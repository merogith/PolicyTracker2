One scale, separate signals

BandRule
No evidence foundCompleted bounded usable-source search found no qualifying evidence. This is not proof of policy absence.
Context documentedRelevant concern, condition, projection, proposal or effect; no attributable response established.
Policy response documentedAttributable commitment or action. Conditions remain visible.
Delivery details documentedSame response has named responsible/implementing actor, action timeframe and a specific verifiable deliverable.

Progress, contradictions and processing completeness stay separate. Bands show evidence presence, not policy adequacy, outcomes or portfolio completeness.
