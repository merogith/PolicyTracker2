# Method sources and design limits

The four documentation bands are a proposed method for this tracker. They are not an official UN, SDG or Sendai indicator or rating.

- The [UN Statistics Division technical note for the 2025 SDG progress assessment](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/files/report/2025/Technical_Note_for_Progress_Assessment_2025.pdf) describes indicator-series trends toward targets and distinguishes insufficient data. Our design borrows clear category-based presentation, not its formulas: policy text does not supply the time-series observations needed to claim SDG progress.
- [UNDRR's description of the Sendai Framework](https://www.undrr.org/implementing-sendai-framework/what-sendai-framework) identifies disaster-risk priorities and substantive global targets. It supports retaining all-hazards risk governance, knowledge, investment and preparedness in the scope. Documentary specificity remains different from disaster outcomes.

Design judgments in this pack—four bands, three delivery checks, topic presence, one home denominator, and two LLM stages—are local recommendations, not requirements attributed to those institutions. Their operational accuracy and cost need evaluation on the supplied documents.

Ordinal band codes are not equal-interval measurements. The response-coverage range represents unresolved topic classification, not a statistical confidence interval. A context-only band is not a criticism of the quality or importance of risk evidence. A detailed response can be conditional, contested or unimplemented; show those qualifications separately.

Source scope matters: one country with many detailed documents can appear better documented than another with a small upload set. Use a common document-role/date profile for comparable views and show exploratory comparisons honestly until it exists. Never infer that missing documentation proves policy absence, non-applicability or low climate ambition.

Sources checked 19 October 2026. Current implementation evidence is local source code, documented in CURRENT-PIPELINE.md and RUNTIME-INPUTS.md.
