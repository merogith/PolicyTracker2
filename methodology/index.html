Methodology

How documents become claims, topic scores, and regional comparisons.

Claim pointsTopicsComparisonsFilters and labelsSource evidence

Claim points

Claim points add the reported progression of distinct claims. Concern, Context, Projection, Result reported, Proposal and Other = 0; Commitment = 1; Planned action = 2; Underway = 3; Delivered = 4.

A topic sums the claims tagged to it. A domain counts each claim once across its home topics; the country counts each claim once overall. Exact restatements with the same figures and statement type are grouped across documents; different wording or progression states stay separate. Multiple tags do not add points. Topic and domain rows can overlap, so their totals must not be added together. Example: two different plans and one delivered claim give 2 + 2 + 4 = 8 points.

Detailed source classifications take precedence over older broad stages. Conflicting progression levels and missing classifications remain unresolved and are excluded from the observed sum. Partial sums are marked provisional and excluded from regional comparisons. Points use the saved claim type; quotation matching is tracked separately, with unmatched evidence flagged for inspection.

These totals measure documented action volume and reported progression. They do not measure policy effectiveness or whether national priorities are appropriate. Countries may have different domain priorities and source collections. Average action stage summarizes the mix of action claims within each topic.

The Action claims measure counts each group once when it includes a selected progression level. The default is stages 1–4. Level 0 includes Concern, Context, Projection, Result reported, Proposal, and Other. Unclassified claims are excluded. Selecting no stages gives zero actions; stage selection does not change claim points.

Average action stage

Average action stage summarizes all scored action claims in a topic. Each distinct claim uses the same stage as the claim-points KPI, scaled to 100:

  1. 25/100Commitment
  2. 50/100Planned action
  3. 75/100Underway
  4. 100/100Delivered

Topic score = action-claim points ÷ action claims × 25. Ten commitments and one delivered claim display as 32/100; one commitment and ten delivered claims display as 93/100. Scores are rounded for display. Each distinct claim counts once, using only evidence tagged to that topic.

Context claims are excluded. A completed topic with no action claims shows “No action documented.” Domain and country scores average the scored home topics with equal topic weights; topics without an action-stage score stay outside that average. Coverage remains a separate measure.

Only source-matched claims with a resolved stage enter the average. Incomplete assessments are provisional and excluded from regional benchmarks. Their averages can rise or fall as evidence is added. This summarizes reported stages, not independently verified delivery.

Coverage and scoring use home topics in enabled domains. Related topics are assessed in their own home domain. With no domain filter, Claims also includes statements outside the assessed topic inventory.

Regional comparisons

Standardized regional score = 100 × Φ(z), where z = (raw total − regional average) ÷ population standard deviation (SD). Φ converts z through the standard normal curve. The regional average maps to 50; −1/+1 SD to 15.9/84.1; −2/+2 SD to 2.3/97.7. Equal totals all receive 50 by convention, even if all raw totals are zero. This is a standardized comparison, not an observed percentile or percentage implementation. Extreme totals affect the average and SD; differences near the ends of the scale are compressed. Each domain or topic is compared with the same domain or topic across at least two complete, non-demo countries. Small groups make the comparison sensitive to individual countries. Colors use the same raw SD bands. Partial results have no standardized score; the reference uses the whole eligible region regardless of display filters.

Only complete, non-demo assessments enter the average. Each country has equal weight. Differences compare countries at this point in time, not progress over time. Source collections and national priorities may differ. A source filter limits the table and average to matching document collections; standardized scores and their colors still use the full eligible region.

Filters and labels

Filters combine across categories. A claim can carry several labels from its supporting findings and appear in several groups. The result count and CSV export count each claim once.

“Other” means no predefined statement type fits. “Not stated / not extracted” means the label is missing. Context, Projection, Result reported, and Proposal are distinct statement types.

Claims with incomplete labels remain searchable, using their older statement type where available. Detailed filters apply to classified evidence. Targets includes only claims classified as targets; missing labels do not establish that targets are absent.

Reported figures retain their stated value, unit, period, baseline, and conditions. Values are not converted or added together. Periods may describe a baseline, reporting period, or target year.

Source evidence and processing

Source links open the cited document page and highlight the quotation. “Text matched” confirms a quotation was found in extracted page text; it does not verify interpretation, implementation, or policy effectiveness. Unmatched quotations remain flagged.

Each document is processed before the next starts for that country. With one document, the overview uses its analysis directly. With several, the combined overview is prepared after their analyses finish.

Text pages are searchable. Scanned pages require OCR. Failed or unfinished analysis is shown as incomplete, not as evidence of no policy.
